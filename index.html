Mic Mute Privacy Policy

Mic Mute, by ByteAcre · Effective 4 November 2026 (version 1.0)

Mic Mute is a Windows app that mutes and unmutes your microphones with one keyboard shortcut and shows whether you are muted. This policy explains what information the app uses and what it does with it. In short: Mic Mute does not record audio, and it does not collect, send or share any of your data. Everything stays on your PC.

Information the app uses

  • Microphone mute setting. Mic Mute reads and changes the Windows mute setting of your microphones, and reads their names (for example "Microphone Array") to show how many are connected. It never opens a microphone and never listens to, records or stores any sound.
  • Keyboard shortcut. Mic Mute registers the shortcut you choose (Ctrl + Alt + M by default) with Windows. If you turn on push-to-talk, it watches only for the one key you picked, to know when it is held down. It does not record, store or send what you type.

Free trial and purchase

Mic Mute is a paid app with a free trial, sold in the Microsoft Store. Purchases are made in Microsoft's own purchase window; ByteAcre never sees your payment details. To know whether you are in the trial or have bought the app, Mic Mute asks the Microsoft Store on your PC for its license. That check is handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement; Mic Mute does not send anything about you with it.

Information stored on your PC

Mic Mute saves the following only on your PC, in the app's own data folder:

  • your settings (shortcut, push-to-talk key, badge position, sound and startup options) and, if you choose, whether you were muted;
  • the last known license state (trial or full), so the app works offline;
  • a small log file of what the app did (for example "muted" or "microphone added"), which helps with troubleshooting. The log never leaves your PC unless you choose to send it to us.

Uninstalling the app removes its data folder.

What the app does not do

  • It does not record or listen to audio.
  • It does not send any information to ByteAcre or anyone else. Its only online activity is the Microsoft Store license and purchase check described above.
  • It does not include advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • It does not access your files, contacts, location or camera.

Start with Windows

If you choose "Start with Windows", the app starts when you sign in. You can turn this off in the app or in Windows Settings > Apps > Startup.

Children

Mic Mute does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
